# US career-page links for H-1B sponsor companies

289 employers. 145 are marked "in feed" -- the scraper reads that company's board, so its roles reach the feed on their own. The other 144 are marked "apply direct": there is no board for them, so the careers link is the only way you will see those roles.

Search this page for "apply direct" to list just the gaps, or "in feed" for just the covered employers. Every entry also carries a United-States-filtered LinkedIn search, which is the reliable way to see only US roles. Native careers links are best-effort: if one redirects, use the LinkedIn link.
