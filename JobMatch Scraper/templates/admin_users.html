What Disable and Delete Actually Do

Disable Ends their session on the next request they make, blocks a fresh sign-in, and revokes their extension token. Reversible.
Revoke Token Invalidates every browser-extension token that account holds. Their web session is untouched; they copy a new token from their profile page.
Delete Removes the account and every row keyed to it: saved jobs, profile, applications, résumés and learned answers. Not reversible, and there is no backup beyond Supabase's own point-in-time recovery.
