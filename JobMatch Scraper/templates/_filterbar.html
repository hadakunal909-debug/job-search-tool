{# THE EXPERIENCE FILTER. It used to be three unlabelled selects under one "Pay & experience" heading, each explaining itself only through a title= nobody hovers, and the experience one mixed two mental models across its four options: "Entry level (0–2 years)" is a range, "Mid level (up to 5 years)" is a ceiling, "Hide senior roles" is a negation. Worse, two of those were the SAME FILTER. app.js drops a job when the years it asks for exceed the selected value, and separately drops anything at 6+ when the value is "senior" — so 5 keeps jobs asking up to 5, and senior keeps jobs asking up to 5. Identical behaviour, two labels, one of them phrased as if it did something else. Reframed as a fact about the READER rather than a rule about jobs, which is what someone actually knows about themselves: how much experience do you have. The stored values are untouched, so nothing about the filtering changes, and a saved "senior" still lands on the option that behaves identically to it. #}
